Sam is an Industrial Organizational psychologist who works as an Innovation Program Manager at a human capital consulting firm. She holds a B.S. from Pennsylvania State University and a M.S. and Ph.D. in Industrial-Organizational Psychology from the University of Oklahoma, where she specialized in the study of creativity and innovation. In addition to publishing research on the topic, she brings deep experience translating that expertise into applied practice.

Sam currently leads a team of consultants delivering customized innovation support to federal clients. She has played a pivotal role in developing and leading a comprehensive innovation program for a federal agency. She runs workshops where employees develop solutions to real challenges and she coaches innovators who carry ideas from submission through development. Sam also advises leadership on innovation strategy and has built the criteria and structure governing boards rely on to evaluate innovates and make funding decisions within their portfolios. Throughout her work, she navigates complex organizational dynamics and works to mobilize people around shared problems.

At the heart of this is a mission Sam is passionate about: helping innovators innovate, and helping leaders lead innovation. That means making the principles of creativity and innovation more accessible, not just to organizations, but to individuals seeking to bring more inventive thinking into their own work and lives.

Originally from New Jersey and now based in Philadelphia, Sam spends her time outside of work traveling to new places, reading science fiction and fantasy, and cuddling her pug Petunia.
